Teen conditions. Aksar miss hoti hain. Hamesha important.

Yeh teeno conditions ke symptoms overlap karte hain — isiliye akele samajhna mushkil hota hai. Hum sab ko saath dekhte hain.

🦋

Hypothyroidism

Thyroid gland ki kami se hormonal imbalance

  • Constant thakaan
  • Weight gain bina wajah
  • Hamesha thand lagna
  • Baal patla hona ya girna
  • Bhoolna / dimag sust rehna
  • Irregular ya heavy periods
🩸

Iron Deficiency Anemia

Khoon mein iron ki kami

  • Kamzori aur saas phoolna
  • Peela chehra ya nakhun
  • Sir dard aur chakkar
  • Baraf / mitti khane ki ichha
  • Baal girna
  • Dinbhar thakaan
🌀

PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome — hormonal disorder

  • Irregular ya band periods
  • Chehre / body pe zyada baal
  • Acne aur oily skin
  • Pet ke around weight gain
  • Pregnancy mein mushkil
  • Mood swings aur anxiety

⚠️ Overlapping symptoms ka problem

Thakaan, baal girna, aur mood swings — yeh teeno conditions mein ho sakte hain. Yahi wajah hai ki akele ek symptom se kuch nahi samajh aata. Synthera saare symptoms ko saath dekhti hai — duration, pattern, lab values — aur phir batati hai kis condition ka risk zyada hai. Saath hi, hum yeh bhi batate hain ki agar thyroid ki problem hai toh uske saath aur kaun si minor health risks ho sakti hain.
