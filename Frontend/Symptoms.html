Step 2 of 4 — Symptoms 50%
Aap kaisi feel kar rahi hain?
Jo bhi symptoms hain wo select karo. Ghabrao mat — apna time lo. Ek se zyada choose kar sakti hain.
💡 Tip: Woh symptoms bhi chunein jo "normal" lagti hain — jaise "aise toh hamesha rehta hai". Kabhi kabhi yahi "normal" lagni wali cheezein actually ek pattern ka hissa hoti hain.
🕐 Yeh symptoms kitne time se hain?
ℹ️ Duration bahut important hai — 2 hafte se zyada ke symptoms zyada significant hote hain medically
📈 Symptoms badh rahe hain ya ek jaise hain?
ℹ️ Worsening symptoms zyada urgent hoti hain — isse hum priority samajhte hain
🏠 Yeh symptoms aapki daily life ko kitna affect kar rahi hain?
ℹ️ Isse hum samajhte hain ki aapko doctor se kitni jaldi milna chahiye
Ab thoda aur detail mein poochti hoon 🩺
Aapne jo symptoms bataye — unke baare mein kuch aur specific sawaal. Yeh prediction ko accurate banate hain.
🧠 Kuch aur important sawaal
Appetite (Bhookh) kaisi rehti hai?
ℹ️ Bhookh mein badlaav thyroid aur PCOS dono se related ho sakti hai
Exercise ya physical activity kaisi hai?
ℹ️ PCOS ke liye physical activity ek key factor hai
Mood kaisa rehta hai aajkal?
ℹ️ Hormonal changes directly mood aur mental health ko affect karte hain
